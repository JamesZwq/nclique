\section{Queries}\label{sec:queries}

\stitle{Value $(v,s)$.}
The bitmap rank gives the chain $c$ of $v$.
If $s>\omega(c)$ the value is $0$; if $s\ge\sigma(c)$ it is $\binomc{\omega(c)-1}{s-1}$ from the binomial table; otherwise it is the residue of $c$ at $s$, at a byte offset given by the chain's residue start and the prefix sum of the per-size widths.
Four memory reads, constant time.

\stitle{Community $(v,s,k)$.}
The chain gives the own node $x=\own{s}{c}$ from the trajectory.
The community is the highest ancestor of $x$ with top at least $k$; the climb follows parent pointers, accelerated by one skip pointer per node in the style of Myers \cite{Myers1983}, so it takes $O(\log d)$ steps for a climb of $d$ levels.
The entries of the reached node and of its successor in preorder then give the answer by Theorem~\ref{thm:retrieval}: a head range, a pointer to the whole runs, and a tail range, in constant time.
Reporting the ranges costs their number; expanding them into vertex labels costs the number of vertices, written eight labels per vector store.

\stitle{Example.}
In Figure~\ref{fig:layout}, the value of $u_1$ at size 3: label 8, the bitmap has three ones up to label 8, so $u_1$ is in chain 2, whose $\omega=3$ and $\sigma=4$; $3<\sigma$, so the answer is the residue of chain 2 at size 3, which is 3.
The value of $a_2$ at size 4: chain 1, $\omega=5$, $\sigma=2\le4$, so the answer is $\binomc{4}{3}=4$ from the table.
The community of $x$ at $(3,2)$: chain 3, whose trajectory gives the own node $N$ at size 3; $N$ has no parent, so the climb stops there; the entries of $N$ and of its successor $A$ are (run 0, label 0) and (run 2, label 3), so the answer is the head $[0,3)$ and the whole run $[8,15)$: $\{b_1,b_2,b_3,u_1,\dots,w_3,x\}$, two ranges for ten vertices.

\stitle{Complexity.}
Value: $O(1)$.
Community: $O(\log d)$ to locate plus the size of the answer in the chosen form.
The bound on $d$ is the depth of $\Tree{s}$, at most the number of distinct core values of size $s$.
