\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this paper, we presented the first index over the nucleus spectrum, the family of $(r,s)$-nucleus core values across a range of cells.
%
By addressing cross-cell certification, exact residue replay, and spectrum storage, we proved two zero-slack transfer theorems and built the one-build sweep algorithm \specnd{} and its index \nsi.
%
On clique-structured graphs, \specnd{} is up to $1{,}600$ times faster than the state-of-the-art per-cell algorithm and uses up to $140$ times less memory, the advantage grows with the clique order, and \nsi{} stores a whole spectrum at as little as $0.001$ bytes per $r$-clique with sub-microsecond queries.
%
On five graphs where per-cell decomposition cannot complete a single cell within a $300$GB budget, \specnd{} computes the entire spectrum in seconds.
%
Our transfer-and-index viewpoint suggests indexing across the clique order as a natural next direction.
